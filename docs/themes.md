# Themes

Eighteen themes, picked in Settings > Appearance and stored per account in `users.palette` (default `plum`). The list lives in `User::PALETTES`.

- `light` (cream), `mac`, `desk95` and `zine` are the light themes (`User::LIGHT_PALETTES`). Every other theme is dark: the layouts set `class="dark"` on `<html>` unless `User::isDarkPalette()` says otherwise (`User::LIGHT_PALETTES`; the same list is in `partials/head.blade.php` and the appearance page), and `app.css` sets `color-scheme` to match.
- Dark palettes only redefine the `zinc-700/800/900/950` tokens. Plum is the base in the `.dark` block; the rest are `.dark[data-palette='…']` overrides in `resources/css/app.css`. Add a palette by adding a CSS block, an entry in `User::PALETTES` and a card in the appearance page.
- Signed-in pages get `data-palette` from the server. Guest pages (sign-in, share links) use the last palette that browser saw, kept in `localStorage` (`ferrite.palette`) by the script in `partials/head.blade.php`.
- `bw` (black and white) also redefines the text tones (`zinc-100…500`) and the accent to neutral greys and white, and swaps the `.cozy-bg` glow and grain for a plain background.
- Colours: terracotta accent, cream text, warm paper grain (`.cozy-bg`). Fonts: Nunito for text, Fraunces for headings (Bunny Fonts via `vite.config.js`).
- `<dialog>` resets its text colour, so `app.css` makes dialogs inherit the page colour. Keep that when touching modals.
- `mac` (Classic Mac, System 6/7 inspired): black on white, 1px black borders, square corners (all `--radius-*` are 0), hard offset shadows (`--shadow-*`), a dotted desktop in `.cozy-bg`, accent black, Pixelify Sans for headings (via `--font-serif`) and Public Sans (a Geneva-like grotesque, so it looks the same on every device) for text. Everything is scoped to `html:not(.dark)[data-palette='mac']` in `app.css`. Not checked in a browser yet; likely to need tweaks (fully rounded avatars and switches are kept round, Flux components with their own colours).
- Fonts per theme: a theme can set `--font-sans` (text), `--font-serif` (headings) and `--font-ui` (buttons, labels, menus, tabs, table heads, sidebar links; inherits the text font when unset). `mac` uses Pixelify Sans (Chicago-like) for headings and `--font-ui` and Public Sans (Geneva-like) for text, like the original Mac. A new theme font must also be added to `vite.config.js`.
- `amber` (Amber terminal): a dark theme in the style of an amber phosphor monitor. Near-black brown surfaces, all text tones redefined in amber, accent `#ffb000`, square corners, glow instead of shadows, scanlines and a faint top glow in `.cozy-bg`, a soft `text-shadow` on the page. IBM Plex Mono for text, VT323 for headings and `--font-ui`. Not checked in a browser yet; VT323 is scaled up with `--font-ui-adjust` (a `font-size-adjust` value, 0.52) because its x-height is small; tune that number.
- `--font-ui-adjust` (optional): a `font-size-adjust` applied to headings and the `--font-ui` elements, to even out fonts with a small x-height. Unset means no adjustment.
- Upload progress bars (`.upload-bar` track, `.upload-fill` fill in the sidebar panel) use the accent colour; `mac` draws a bordered bar with a black diagonal-stripe fill and `amber` a bar of amber blocks. Give a new theme its own look by overriding these two classes in `app.css`.
- `phosphor` (Green phosphor) and `amber` share one block (`.dark:is([data-palette='amber'], [data-palette='phosphor'])`) and differ in colour tokens and `--crt-glow` (the phosphor as an `r g b` triplet). Both remap `--color-white`.
- `desk95` (Desktop 95, light): silver surfaces, navy accent, square corners, raised bevels (box-shadow, not borders) on buttons that have a background, dialogs and popovers, sunken bevels on inputs, progress bar of navy blocks. Public Sans (Tahoma-like).
- `zine` (Plain page, light): early-web look. White page, Tinos (Times-like) text, Courier Prime for buttons and labels, blue underlined links, no shadows or rounding.
- `commodore` (dark): C64 start screen. Dark blue paper, light blue text, yellow-green accent, a light blue frame drawn by `html::after` (fixed, `pointer-events: none`, very high z-index), DotGothic16 for text and Press Start 2P for headings and `--font-ui` (scaled down with `--font-ui-adjust: 0.4`).
- `amiga` (dark): Workbench 1.3. Blue screen, white text, orange accent, black hard shadows, Share Tech Mono text and Silkscreen headings and `--font-ui`.
- The four newest themes (`desk95`, `zine`, `commodore`, `amiga`) and `phosphor` are unchecked in a browser. Likely rough spots: Flux components with their own colours, `desk95` bevels on elements I did not target, and Press Start 2P / Silkscreen at small sizes.
